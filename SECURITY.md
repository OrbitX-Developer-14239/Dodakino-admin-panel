## 📣 Reporting a Vulnerability

If you discover a security vulnerability in Doda Kino Admin Panel or any of its components, we take it **very seriously** and appreciate your efforts to report it responsibly.

Please follow these steps:

1. **Report Privately:**
   Send an email to [orbitx.team@gmail.com](mailto:orbitx.team@gmail.com) with the details of the issue.

2. **Include the Following Information:**
   - A detailed description of the vulnerability.
   - Steps to reproduce the issue.
   - Environment details (browser/OS if relevant).
   - Potential impact or risk level (e.g., XSS, CSRF, RCE, etc.).

3. **Our Response:**
   - You will receive an acknowledgment within **48 hours**.
   - If the vulnerability is confirmed, we will work on a fix within **7 days**.
   - Responsible reporters may be credited (optional) in our **Security Contributors** list.

---

## ⚠️ Important Notes

- **Please do NOT open public issues** for security reports.
- All valid reports are handled confidentially until patched.
- We appreciate ethical, responsible disclosures and treat reporters with respect.
